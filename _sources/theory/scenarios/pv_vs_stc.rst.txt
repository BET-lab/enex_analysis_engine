Photovoltaic vs. Solar Thermal Collector
========================================

This section compares the performance of Photovoltaic (PV) panels and Solar Thermal Collectors (STC) under equal-area conditions. The analysis evaluates both the Energy Yield and the Exergy Yield to provide a comprehensive thermodynamic assessment.

Energy and Exergy Analysis
--------------------------

While the energy efficiency of an STC is typically higher than that of a PV panel, exergy analysis reveals a different perspective. Exergy accounts for the quality of energy. Electrical energy from PV panels represents pure exergy (quality factor $\approx 1$), whereas the low-grade thermal energy produced by STCs has a lower exergy value.

The results illustrate the 'thermodynamic irrationality' in certain scenarios where solely focusing on energy yield might lead to suboptimal utilization of available solar exergy.
